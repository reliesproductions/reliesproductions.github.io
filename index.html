Mid-Day Meal Calculator

App for Android, education

ಬಿಸಿಯೂಟ ಲೆಕ್ಕ, in Kannada, Hindi and English

For Karnataka headmasters and teachers who run the PM POSHAN mid-day meal. Enter the day's attendance and the app works out the rice, dal, oil and other quantities, keeps the stock register and builds the monthly report.

  • The daily calculation takes minutes instead of an hour
  • Monthly reports as PDF and Excel
  • Works offline, with the data kept on the phone

Bus Simulator: Twilight Route

Game for Android, simulation

Drive city buses and long-distance coaches through four cities built from real street maps, in a hand-painted anime look where every trip ends in golden hour.

  • Nine detailed buses, inside and out

The last member leaves. The accounts are already closed.

Gym owners work the longest days in town. GymChief keeps the fees, receipts and reminders so they can lock up and go home.

GymChief: Gym Management

App for Android, business

Members, fees, receipts and plans in one app that lives on the phone. See who has paid, who is due and whose membership ends this week, then send the reminder on WhatsApp in one tap.

  • Admission and payment receipts, ready to share
  • Workout and diet plans for each member
  • Private by design: encrypted, offline, no accounts

The studio

One small studio, under the same sky as everyone it builds for.

Relies Productions is an independent app and game studio founded by Ramachand Sanjay Nayak in Ankola, Uttara Kannada. We design, build and publish our own Android apps and games.

We keep our apps simple to use, honest about data and useful from the first day. Where we can, the information you enter stays on your own phone.
